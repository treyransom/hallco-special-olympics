Get involved

There's a place for you here.

Athletes, families, coaches, partners, and fans. It takes all of us.

Become an athlete

Anyone age 8 and up with an intellectual disability can compete. No experience needed, no cost ever.

Register

Volunteer or coach

Keep score, run a water station, or lead a team for a season. We'll train you and you'll leave with a bigger family.

Sign up

Be a unified partner

Play on the same team as our athletes in flag football, bowling, and more. Inclusion happens on the field.
